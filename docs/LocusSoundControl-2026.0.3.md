## 2026.0.3

### Added
- The menu bar icon shows what you’re listening through, and changes as the output moves between your speakers, headphones, dock or display.
- Clicking the menu bar icon lists the output devices, each with its own icon, and checks the one in use. Picking one doesn’t switch to it yet — that comes in a later release.
- The Sound Devices window lists what it found: how each device is connected, and the identifiers it is known by.
- Devices appear and disappear as you connect and unplug them. After waking from sleep or docking, Locus Sound Control waits for the list to stop changing instead of reacting to every step.
- AirPods, AirPods Pro, AirPods Max, Powerbeats and most of the Beats range get their own icon, including models released after this build. Anything else is recognized by how it is attached — Bluetooth headphones, a Bluetooth speaker, car audio, a display, or the Mac’s own speakers.
- Locus Sound Control asks for Bluetooth access the first time you play through a Bluetooth device. It is used to tell a Bluetooth speaker from headphones, so the icon is right, and for nothing else. It never scans for or connects to devices, and saying no only means those devices show a headphones icon.

### Changed
- The Sound Devices window opens centered on your main display the first time, and reopens where you left it after that.
